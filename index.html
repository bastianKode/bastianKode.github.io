The studio

About

I’m Bastian B., a 3D perfume bottle and packaging designer. I model original bottles to real-world scale and light them in a studio so brands can see a concept clearly — before tooling, before a photoshoot, and often before the juice has a name.

Work covers luxury crystal, modern minimalist, geometric and classic ornate styles. You receive named, separate parts (glass, liquid, collar, cap or stopper), physically based materials, and files ready for mockups, e-commerce, investor decks or a manufacturer briefing.

Send a brief, a mood board or a sketch. I’ll recommend a package and turn it into a polished bottle concept, with fast turnaround and revisions included.
